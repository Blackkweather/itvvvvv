# Multi-Country Landing Pages Structure

To maximize SEO reach and conversions, we'll create country-specific versions of our main landing page. These pages will target specific regions with localized content while maintaining the same core structure.

## Country-Specific Page Structure

Each country page will be located at:
`/best-iptv-[country]-subscription-plan`

Examples:
- `/best-iptv-usa-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-uk-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-canada-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-australia-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-germany-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-france-subscription-plan`
- `/best-iptv-spain-subscription-plan`

## Key Elements for Country Pages

### 1. Localized Headlines
- "Best IPTV Subscription Plan for USA Live TV & Sports 2026"
- "Meilleur Abonnement IPTV pour la Télévision et les Sports en France 2026"
- "Beste IPTV-Abos für Live-TV und Sport in Deutschland 2026"

### 2. Region-Specific Content Sections
- Local sports leagues (NFL, NBA for USA; Premier League for UK; Bundesliga for Germany)
- Local payment methods (PayPal, credit cards, local payment options)
- Local customer support availability
- Local pricing in regional currency (where applicable)

### 3. Country-Specific Keywords
- "best iptv usa 2026"
- "meilleur iptv france 2026"
- "beste iptv deutschland 2026"
- "iptv españa 2026"

### 4. Localized Testimonials
- Customer reviews from each specific country
- Highlight local sports teams/channels popularity

### 5. Country-Specific FAQ Section
- Questions about legality in that country
- Local setup guides
- Regional customer support information
